In plainer language: once the bridge opens on one side and the floor opens on the other, the layer in the middle becomes impossible to ignore.

In plainer language: one thing can show up in different ways without becoming many unrelated things.

Chapter 5 — The Planck Floor

1. Why a Floor Is Needed at All
The first step is simple: if Density is bounded holdability, then it cannot run continuously to zero.
That conclusion follows directly from the word bounded.
A layer of pure holdability with no lower limit would allow arbitrarily small, arbitrarily weak, arbitrarily thin “holdings” of interaction-conditioned structure. But a Boundary that can be thinned without limit would eventually cease to be a Boundary in any meaningful sense. At some point there would no longer be enough structure to preserve an inside/outside distinction, not enough structure to carry consequence outward, and not enough structure to resist immediate erasure under entropy.

In plainer language: if Density is really bounded, then there has to be a point below which it can no longer hold anything as a real Boundary.

2. The Two Faces Boundary Must Hold
The next step is to say what exactly is supposed to remain real at that minimum scale.
Boundary is not a one-sided object. It has two load-bearing faces.
The first is distinction-face. Boundary preserves inside/outside separation. It gives a system a genuine interior rather than a mere notional point.
The second is re-expression-face. Boundary does not only separate. It also makes consequence outwardly carryable. What persists inside a Boundary must still be capable, at least minimally, of entering outward consequence rather than remaining sealed in inert isolation.
These two faces are not optional add-ons. They are part of what makes Boundary Boundary at all.
That matters because the minimum scale in question is not just a minimum size. It is the minimum scale at which both of these faces can still be load-bearing at the same time.
In plainer language: the floor is not just the smallest possible “inside.” It is the smallest scale at which inside/outside distinction and outward consequence can still both be real.
This is the chapter’s central structural shift. It moves the Planck problem from “smallness” to “simultaneous face viability.”
3. The Distinction-Face Floor

The distinction-face requires a genuine interior. That means it requires some minimum geometric extent. A putative Boundary with zero extent has no real inside. It does not separate anything. It names difference in word only, not in held structure.
So the distinction-face cannot remain operative all the way to zero length.
At some point, the geometric scale becomes too thin for an interior to remain meaningfully interior. The result is not a very small Boundary. The result is collapse of distinction itself.
In plainer language: if there is no real room for an inside, there is no Boundary.
That already gives us one side of the floor. There must be a minimum geometric extent below which distinction-face fails.
The paper does not yet need, at this step, to state the final value of that extent. It only needs to show that such a limit exists.
4. The Re-Expression-Face Floor

The re-expression-face requires that consequence be able to exit in a holdable way. Not just “occur,” but remain coherent enough to propagate outward rather than dissolving immediately.
